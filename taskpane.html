How to lay out your data
  • Top row: period labels (Jan, Q1, 2026 …). Leave the corner cell empty.
  • One row per scenario, starting with its code: AC actual · FC forecast · PL plan · BU budget · PY last year.
  • Blank cell = no value. In the example, actuals run to Feb and the forecast takes over from Mar — the chart shows solid columns, then hatched.
  • Sideways data works too: scenario codes across the top row, periods down the first column. The pane detects the orientation automatically.
  • Friendly names are fine: Actual, Plan, Budget, Forecast, Last year are recognised and normalised. For small multiples, label the rows: AC North, AC South, …
Select the whole block, header row included, then press Load selection.

Inserted charts are plain pictures, so they display for anyone — add-in or not. While the add-in is running they refresh automatically when their source cells change; on Microsoft 365 it keeps running in the background for as long as the workbook is open, even after you close this pane. Refresh all catches up on edits made while the add-in was not running.
